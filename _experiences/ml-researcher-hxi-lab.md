At UCSD's Human-centered eXtended Intelligence (HXI) Lab, I work on training language models to produce more emotionally regulated responses — output that reflects healthier cognitive emotion regulation (CERQ) rather than just plausible-sounding text.

---

#### 🔍 What I Worked On

- **Preference-pair generation**: Built Reddit-based pipelines to generate preference pairs across CERQ dimensions using Vertex AI, improving classification by **21 percentage points**.
- **DPO fine-tuning**: Fine-tuned transformer models on **100K+ samples** using Direct Preference Optimization on **4x A100 Nautilus clusters**.
- **Human evaluation**: Validated the fine-tuned models against human judgment, with responses preferred in **72% of evaluations** across regulated and dysregulated variants.

---

#### 💡 Why It's Relevant

Most LLM alignment work optimizes for helpfulness or harmlessness in the abstract. This project is narrower and more concrete: can a model's responses be steered toward a specific, clinically-grounded notion of emotional regulation, and can that be measured against real human judgment rather than a proxy metric?
